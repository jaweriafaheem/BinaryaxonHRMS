Introduction
Prime HR is a comprehensive, cloud-based Human Resource Management System (HRMS) designed to streamline and automate every dimension of the employee lifecycle — from onboarding and daily attendance to payroll processing and final settlement. Built for multi-company, multi-location enterprises, Prime HR consolidates HR operations into a single, role-driven platform accessible by administrators, managers, and employees alike.

This document provides a bird's-eye view of all functional modules available in Prime HR, summarising the sub-modules and key features within each. It is intended for stakeholders, implementation teams, and new users who need a concise map of system capabilities.
